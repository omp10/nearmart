Delete your Nearmart account

Applies to the Nearmart customer app, rider app and seller accounts.

How to ask

  1. Open the Nearmart app and go to Help or Contact us.
  2. Ask to delete my account, from the mobile number registered on the account (for sellers, the registered mobile number or email).
  3. To protect your account, we confirm the request with that number before deleting anything.

We complete the deletion within 30 days and tell you when it is done. Any orders in progress are completed or cancelled first. A seller's or rider's money still owed is paid out before the account is closed.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, email and profile photo
  • Saved addresses and map pins
  • Cart, preferences and notification (device) tokens
  • Sign-in sessions. You are signed out on all devices.
  • For riders: the stored location and account details. For sellers: the store profile, listings, and the bank and KYC details that the law does not require us to keep.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Order, invoice, payment, refund and payout records are kept as long as Indian tax and accounting laws require (generally up to 8 years). They are used only for that purpose and for resolving disputes.
  • Records needed to prevent fraud or to meet a legal obligation are kept only as long as necessary.
