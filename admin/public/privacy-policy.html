Privacy Policy

Effective 31 October 2026

Nearmart ("Nearmart", "we", "us") runs the Nearmart customer app, the delivery partner (rider) app, the seller panel and the admin panel (together, the "Services"). We sell and deliver groceries, fresh vegetables and fruit, and electrical and electronics items, and we run laundry pickup and delivery. Some products are sold by independent sellers through Nearmart.

This policy explains what personal data we collect, why we use it, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and the choices you have. It is written to meet the Information Technology Act, 2000, its rules, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. By using the Services you agree to this policy.

1. What we collect

1.1 Customers

DataWhy we use it
Mobile numberTo sign you in with a one-time password (OTP), to identify your account, and to contact you about your orders.
Name, and email and profile photo if you add themTo show on your account and orders and to address you.
Delivery and pickup addresses (house, street, landmark, city, state, PIN code), with the contact name and phone for each, and an optional map pin (latitude and longitude)To check that we deliver to your area, to choose the nearest store, delivery fee and slots, and to deliver your order or collect and return your laundry.
Orders and laundry bookings: items, quantities, prices, coupons, delivery or pickup slot, instructions, status history, ratings or issues you reportTo process, deliver, support, cancel or refund your order, to keep tax and accounting records, and to improve our range and service.
Cart and app preferences (for example, notification settings)To keep your cart between visits and to respect your choices.
Payment details: payment method, amount, payment and refund IDs and statusTo take payment and issue refunds. Card, UPI and bank details are entered with our payment gateway (Razorpay); Nearmart never sees or stores your full card number, CVV or UPI PIN.
Device information: push notification token, platform (Android/iOS/web), app version, IP address and device/browser type at sign-inTo send order updates, to keep your account secure (for example, signing out other devices) and to prevent fraud and abuse.

1.2 Delivery partners (riders)

DataWhy we use it
Name, mobile number, vehicle type, the shops you deliver forTo create your account, sign you in by OTP, and assign tasks.
Live location (GPS), sent about every 15 seconds only while you are online in the rider app, including in the background during that timeTo assign nearby tasks, let the store see where deliveries are, and resolve delivery disputes. Location is not collected while you are offline.
Tasks and their status, cash on delivery collected and deposited, earnings and payoutsTo run deliveries, settle cash and pay you.
Device push token and sign-in informationTo send new task alerts and keep your account secure.

1.3 Sellers

DataWhy we use it
Business name, owner name, mobile number, email, password (stored only as a one-way hash)To create and sign in to your seller account and contact you.
Store address and map location, store description, logo and photos, opening hours, holidays, service areasTo show your store to customers, decide where you deliver, and route orders.
GSTIN, PAN, FSSAI licence number, and KYC documents you upload (for example GST certificate, PAN card, FSSAI licence, shop licence, cancelled cheque, ID proof)To verify your business, meet legal and tax requirements, and prevent fraud. Documents are kept in private storage that is not publicly reachable, and only authorised Nearmart staff can view them.
Bank account holder name, account number, IFSC and UPI IDTo pay out your earnings. The details used are recorded with each payout.
Products, prices, stock, orders, commission, adjustments and payoutsTo run the marketplace and keep accounts.

1.4 Nearmart staff

Name, work email, phone, password (stored as a hash), role, permissions and sign-in history, to give access to the admin panel and keep it secure.

1.5 What we do not collect

We do not read your contacts, messages, call logs, photos (other than images you choose to upload) or files. We do not collect location from customers in the background. We do not sell personal data.

2. Where the data comes from

Almost all of it comes from you, when you sign up, place an order or use the app. Some is created as you use the Services (orders, delivery status, rider location). Payment status comes from Razorpay. A seller's or rider's details may be entered by Nearmart staff when setting up the account.

3. Who we share it with

We share only what each party needs to do its job:

  • Sellers see the items, delivery details and name and phone needed to prepare and hand over your order.
  • Delivery partners see your name, phone, address and map pin for the orders they deliver or collect, and only while the task is assigned.
  • Service providers that process data for us under contract: Razorpay (payments and refunds), SMS India Hub (OTP text messages), Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (push notifications), Google Maps Platform (maps, address search and pins), and our cloud hosting provider (servers and storage).
  • Authorities when required by law, court order or to protect the rights, safety or property of our users or Nearmart, including tax authorities for GST records.
  • A buyer or successor if Nearmart is merged, acquired or sells its business, under the same protections.

We do not share personal data with advertisers and we do not sell it.

4. How long we keep it

  • Account details: while your account is active. After you delete your account (see section 7), we delete or anonymise them within 30 days.
  • Orders, invoices, payments and payouts: as long as tax and accounting laws require (generally up to 8 years), even after the account is deleted. These records are kept only for that purpose.
  • OTP codes: a few minutes until they expire. Sign-in sessions: until you sign out or they expire (up to 30 days).
  • Rider location: the latest position is kept on the rider's account, and positions are not kept as a history.
  • Seller KYC documents: while the seller account is active and afterwards as long as the law requires.

5. How we protect it

All traffic uses HTTPS. Passwords are stored only as bcrypt hashes. Payment gateway keys are encrypted, and seller documents are kept outside public storage. Admin access is limited by role and module, and sessions end at once when a password changes or an account is switched off. Sign-in attempts and OTP requests are rate-limited. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your data, we will inform you and the authorities as the law requires.

6. Permissions the apps ask for

  • Location: customer app, while in use, to find your address and nearest store (optional; you can type your address instead). Rider app: precise location while online, including in the background during that time, to run deliveries.
  • Notifications: order, booking and task updates. You can turn these off in your phone's settings or the app.
  • Camera / photos: only when you choose to upload a picture (for example, a profile photo or a product image).

7. Your rights and choices

You can:

  • see and correct your name, email, addresses and preferences in the app;
  • ask for a copy of your personal data, or ask us to correct it;
  • delete your account and the personal data linked to it. See how to delete your account;
  • withdraw consent (for example, for notifications or location) at any time. Some features may then stop working;
  • name another person to exercise these rights for you if you die or are unable to;
  • complain to our Grievance Officer (section 10), and if you are not satisfied, to the Data Protection Board of India.

8. Children

The Services are for people aged 18 or over. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed. For important changes we will tell you in the app before they take effect.

10. Contact and Grievance Officer

For questions, requests or complaints about your data, use Contact us or Help in the Nearmart app, or write to our Grievance Officer using the details shown there. We acknowledge complaints within 48 hours and resolve them within 30 days.
